Single Centralized Acknowledgment Workflow

Anti-Matrix operates a streamlined, single-email communication workflow. When a candidate submits an application (and completes payment where applicable), this confirmation email is automatically scheduled and dispatched after exactly 5 minutes. This email confirms receipt, clarifies that the candidate’s application is under active review, and informs them that they will receive future communications only if shortlisted. Separate automated emails for shortlisting, rejection, assessment, or interview invitations are not active at this stage.
